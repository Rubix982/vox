    Models are fetched from Hugging Face's CDN once and cached in browser storage (IndexedDB). No telemetry, no analytics, no audio uploads. Whisper: onnx-community/whisper-large-v3-turbo on WebGPU (~800 MB, q4 quantized) — falls back to Xenova/whisper-base.en if WebGPU is unavailable.
